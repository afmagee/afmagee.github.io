## About me

I am a [DDLS fellow](https://www.scilifelab.se/data-driven/), [MIMS group leader](https://www.umu.se/mims/), and member of the [IceLab](https://www.umu.se/en/icelab/) at the Umeå University [Department of Molecular Biology](https://www.umu.se/en/department-of-molecular-biology/).
Previously, I have been a data scientist with the [Center for Forecasting and Outbreak Analytics](https://www.cdc.gov/forecast-outbreak-analytics/index.html) at the CDC and a postdoc at UCLA in [Marc Suchard's](https://suchard-group.github.io) group.
I obtained my PhD candidate from the University of Washington, Seattle, co-advised by [Vladimir Minin](https://vnminin.github.io/) and [Erick Matsen](https://matsen.fhcrc.org/).
If you have questions about phylogenetics, feel free to send me an email.
